A CN3302 charger board: a step-up (boost) charger that takes 5 V from a USB-C
port and charges two lithium cells in series to 8.4 V. In the chassis it
charges the six-cell 18650 pack, wired as two groups of three parallel cells in series. It sits
on the floor in front of the battery pack, beside the IP6505 board, with its
USB-C port against the inside of the front wall, behind a hole for the plug.
