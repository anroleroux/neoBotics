A lid that fits on top of the base box (PB4/chassis/base). It extends past
the box's breadth by its own thickness on each side and runs down the outside
of the box to the bottom of the base. In the longitudinal direction it extends
a = 5 mm past the box at both ends. Under the lid, two lips sit at the
front (+x) and the back (-x), just inside the base's end walls; there are no
lips along the sides (+-y).
