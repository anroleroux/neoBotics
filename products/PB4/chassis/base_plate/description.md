A flat chassis base plate, 160 mm long, 120 mm wide and 4 mm thick, with its
four corners rounded to a 10 mm radius. Four M4 clearance holes, one near each
corner, 10 mm in from both edges, bolt it to the standoffs above. A cable
slot, 40 mm long and 12 mm wide with fully rounded ends, runs along the
length through the centre of the plate. Two motor-mount patterns, one on each
long side, each have two M3 clearance holes 20 mm apart along the length,
centred on the plate's length and 12 mm in from the long edge.
