A rectangular box that is open at the top. Four variables drive its size: l
(length), b (breadth), h (height) and the wall thickness. The walls and the
floor are all one wall thickness thick. x points along the length, y along
the breadth and z up the height. The sides at +y and -y are removed and the
sides at +x and -x are kept, so the box is also open along both long sides.

The floor carries five battery separator extrusions that run along x between
the end walls and rise from the floor: three between the four 18650 cells,
keeping neighbouring cells apart, and two side battery retainers, one along
each open long side, holding the outer cells in.
