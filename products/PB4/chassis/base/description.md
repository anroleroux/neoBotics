A rectangular box that is open at the top. Four variables drive its size: l
(length), b (breadth), h (height) and the wall thickness. The walls and the
floor are all one wall thickness thick. x points along the length, y along
the breadth and z up the height. The sides at +y and -y are removed and the
sides at +x and -x are kept, so the box is also open along both long sides.

The floor carries five battery separator extrusions that run along x and rise
from the floor, 4 mm higher than first modelled: three between the four 18650
cells, keeping neighbouring cells apart, and two side battery retainers, one
along each open long side, holding the outer cells in. A small wall in front
of the batteries, as high as the extrusions, runs across the breadth. The two
side retainers run from the back wall to this front mini wall. The centre
separator runs from 1 mm in front of the back wall to the front mini wall.
The other two separators stop 1 mm short of the back wall and end at the
front faces of the batteries.
