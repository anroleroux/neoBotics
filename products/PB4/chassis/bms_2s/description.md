The 2S 7.4V 8A 18650 lithium battery protection board (BMS) from Robofactory:
a long, narrow board, 38 x 8 x 2.6 mm, that protects a two-cell-series pack
against overcharge (4.25 to 4.35 V per cell), over-discharge (2.5 to 3.0 V
per cell), overcurrent and short circuits. In the chassis it stands on edge
against the front face of the front mini wall, with its top face, which
carries the components, facing the front.
