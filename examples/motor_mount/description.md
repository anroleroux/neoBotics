An L-shaped mounting bracket for a NEMA 17 stepper motor. A 3 mm thick
vertical plate, 50 mm wide and 50 mm tall, holds the motor face: a 22.5 mm
centre bore for the motor's pilot boss and four M3 clearance holes on the
NEMA 17 31 mm square pattern, centred on the bore. A 3 mm thick base plate,
50 mm wide and 40 mm deep, runs back from the bottom of the vertical plate
and is screwed down with two M4 holes, 30 mm apart, centred 25 mm behind the
vertical plate. Two triangular gussets, 3 mm thick, brace the corner at the
outer edges. Round the corner where the plates meet with a 3 mm inside fillet.
