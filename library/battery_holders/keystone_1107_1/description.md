A Keystone 1107-1 battery clip with a solder lug, in tin-nickel plated
spring steel, for AA, A, 17500, 18350, 18650, C and D cells. It is a flat plate
0.250 in (6.35 mm) wide and 0.538 in (13.7 mm) tall. A solder lug is cut from
the plate and bent out at an angle, reaching 0.156 in (4.0 mm) from the
plate's back; its end is 0.300 in (7.6 mm) below the plate's top, and it has
a 0.070 in (1.8 mm) solder hole.

At the top, a jog bends the sheet 180 degrees all the way back down, on the
opposite side of the plate from the solder lug: the spring the cell presses
on. The fold leaves a 1 mm gap between the plate and the spring, so the clip
hooks over a holder's 1 mm end wall. It is drawn in its deformed shape, folded
flat against the cell, although in the free state it does not bend the full
180 degrees.
