The plastic body of a single 18650 cell holder: a hollow rectangular prism,
open on the top and bottom sides, with a variable wall thickness of 1 mm by
default. Two ribs across the inside, spaced along the length of the battery,
each have a semicircular cut-out that the battery lies in.

Triangular extrusions run along the bottom on either side of the holder,
from the very front face to the very end. Their profile is flush with the
bottom, goes up along the sides, and tapers off at 45 degrees to the bottom
edge close to the centre; the 45 degree face is about 1 mm outside the arcs
of the ribs.
