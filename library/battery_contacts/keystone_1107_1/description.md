A Keystone 1107-1 battery clip with a solder lug, in tin-nickel plated
spring steel, for AA, A, 17500, 18350, 18650, C and D cells. It is a flat plate
0.250 in (6.4 mm) wide and 0.538 in (13.7 mm) tall, with a 0.070 in (1.8 mm)
solder hole near the top. A spring leaf is cut from the plate and bent out
into a V that stands 0.156 in (4.0 mm) proud of the plate's back, its apex
0.300 in (7.6 mm) below the plate's top, and presses on the cell's end.
