spring steel, for AA, A, 17500, 18350, 18650, C and D cells. It is a flat plate
0.250 in (6.4 mm) wide and 0.538 in (13.7 mm) tall. A solder lug is cut from

plate's back; its end is 0.300 in (7.6 mm) below the plate's top, and it has
a 0.070 in (1.8 mm) solder hole. The spring that presses on the cell's end is
